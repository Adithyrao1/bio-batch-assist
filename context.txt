## Comprehensive Application Summary

### **1. APPLICATION OVERVIEW**

**DCM LabNest** is a **DCM SHRIRAM Tissue Culture Data Management System** for managing Sugarcane tissue culture lab operations. It's a web-based application for tracking production batches, contamination monitoring, chemical inventory, and greenhouse operations across multiple specialized lab areas.

---

### **2. ALL ENTITIES & DATA MODELS**

#### **Primary Entities:**

1. **Contamination Monitoring**
   - Tracks contamination detection across areas
   - Fields: ID, dateTime, area, platesExposed, observationDateTime, colonyCount, colonyType, actionTaken
   - Colony types: Fungal, Bacterial, Yeast, None

2. **Media Preparation**
   - Manages prepared culture media batches
   - Fields: ID, batchNumber, prepDate, quantity, bottlesPrepared, preparedBy, contaminationNotes, bottlesIssued, issuedDate, mediaType
   - Media types: MS Medium, B5 Medium, White's Medium, N6 Medium

3. **Growth Room**
   - Daily tracking of culture growth and inventory management
   - Fields: ID, varietyCode, ltdDate (logged transaction date), planning, openingBottles, openingCultures, issuedBottles, issuedCultures, receivedBottles, receivedCultures, contaminatedBottles, contaminatedCultures, closingBottles, closingCultures
   - Represents material flow in/out/contamination within growth rooms

4. **Inoculation Room**
   - Records inoculation operations and production data
   - Fields: ID, variety, date, operator, cultures, bottles, totalProduced, remarks
   - Tracks yields and quality notes per operation

5. **Chemicals**
   - Chemical inventory management with shelf-life tracking
   - Fields: ID, name, quantity, unit, mfgDate, expiryDate, receivedDate, remainingStock
   - Automatic expiry detection (red flag if past expiryDate)

6. **Contamination Reports**
   - Aggregated contamination incident records
   - Fields: ID, varietyCode, source (Fungus/Bacteria), typeDesc, bottlesAffected, operator, date
   - Links inoculation/growth failures to specific incidents

7. **Greenhouse**
   - Greenhouse operations and plant observations
   - Fields: ID, variety, batchNumber, transplantDate, operationDate, operationDesc, observationDate, findings (array), plantletsDied, recordedBy
   - Findings: Healthy, New Roots, Wilting, Yellowing, Pest Damage, Stunted Growth
   - Tracks mortality and health status

8. **Users**
   - User account management
   - Fields: ID, name, username, role, status (active/inactive)
   - Seed data: Vineeta Raina (admin), Kajal (technician), Satyam (technician), Anurag (viewer)

9. **Master Data (Reference)**
   - Areas: 6 lab/greenhouse locations
   - Varieties: 5 sugarcane varieties (SC-001 through SC-005)
   - Media Types: 4 standard media formulations
   - Findings: 6 observation categories for greenhouse

10. **Dashboard Metrics (Derived)**
    - Contamination heatmap by area
    - Contamination trend (monthly)
    - Production trend (batches by month)
    - Variety distribution (pie)
    - Growth stage distribution
    - Yield success rates by variety
    - Chemical burn rate tracking

### **3. ENTITY RELATIONSHIPS**

```
Variety (SC-001, SC-002, etc.)
├─ relates to → Contamination Reports
├─ relates to → Growth Room (daily tracking)
├─ relates to → Inoculation Room (production)
└─ relates to → Greenhouse (hardening/observation)

Media Preparation (batch)
└─ consumed by → Growth Room & Inoculation Room operations

Chemical (inventory)
└─ used in → Media Preparation & Growth operations

Contamination Monitoring (detection event)
├─ may result in → Contamination Report
└─ triggers → Action Taken (UV sterilization, deep cleaning)

Operator/User
├─ prepares → Media Preparation batches
├─ operates → Inoculation Room production
├─ records → Growth Room daily transactions
├─ detects → Contamination incidents
└─ observes → Greenhouse findings

Area (physical location)
├─ has → Contamination Monitoring events
└─ manages → Inoculation Rooms A/B, Growth Rooms 1/2, Media Prep Lab, Greenhouse
```

### **5. MAIN WORKFLOWS & FEATURES**

#### **Workflow 1: Media Production & Distribution**
1. Technician prepares media batch (Media Preparation)
   - Records: batch #, prep date, quantity, bottles prepared, media type, contamination notes
2. Media issued to Growth Room
   - Tracked: bottlesIssued, issuedDate
3. Growth Room consumes media in daily operations
   - Logged: daily transaction with opening/closing inventory

#### **Workflow 2: Inoculation & Culture Production**
1. Operator performs inoculation in Inoculation Room
   - Records: date, varieties inoculated, cultures produced, bottles output, remarks
2. Produces: plantlets/cultures for downstream growth

#### **Workflow 3: Contamination Detection & Response**
1. Contamination Monitoring detects incident
   - Records: date/time, area, plates exposed, colony count, type
2. May trigger Contamination Report
   - Records: variety affected, contamination source, bottles affected
3. Action taken: UV sterilization, deep cleaning, fumigation
4. Dashboard displays heatmap of contamination by area

#### **Workflow 4: Growth & Scaling**
1. Growth Room receives cultures from Inoculation
   - Daily log: opening inventory, issued out, received, contaminated, closing inventory
2. Subculture cycles tracked by variety
3. Growth stage progression: Inoculation → Growth Room → Greenhouse → Hardening

#### **Workflow 5: Greenhouse Hardening & Observation**
1. Plantlets transplanted from growth room
   - Records: transplant date, batch number
2. Operations applied: hardening, misting adjustment
3. Observations recorded: health status, findings, mortality count
4. Success tracking for yield analysis

#### **Workflow 6: Chemical Inventory & Stock Control**
1. Chemicals received and logged
   - Tracks: mfg date, expiry date, remaining stock
2. System flags expired chemicals (red alert)
3. Dashboard shows burn rate (consumption per week)

#### **Workflow 7: User & System Administration** (Admin only)
1. Manage master data: Areas, Varieties, Media Types, Findings
2. Manage users: Create, edit, deactivate
3. All data modifications require admin role
